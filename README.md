## Chosen dataset
Obesity constitutes a significant global public health burden, associated with elevated risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, and psychological comorbidities, with prevalence rates continuing to rise worldwide every year. Identifying the behavioral and physiological determinants of obesity is therefore critical for developing effective prevention and megical intervention strategies.

This dataset was selected as it provides a multivariate representation of eating and drinking habits, physical activity levels, and demographic characteristics across individuals from Mexico, Peru, and Colombia, enabling systematic investigation of the factors associated with obesity and supporting the development of classification models across seven weight status categories.
